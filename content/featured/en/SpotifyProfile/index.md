This is an interactive car configurator built with HTML, CSS, and jQuery. Users can browse different car models, customize colors, and see dynamic price updates in real-time. The interface is sleek and user-friendly, providing a visually appealing experience where changes to models, colors, and features are immediately reflected on the screen.
